      10 nm trap:    92 rad^2 of deflection per fusion mean free path (direction randomised ~92 times per fusion)
    1000 nm trap:   121 rad^2 of deflection per fusion mean free path (direction randomised ~121 times per fusion)
  energy handed to boron per fusion mean free path: 8.4 x the proton's own energy
  beam trap that keeps only protons within a cone, ideal accelerator, 70 % direct conversion:
    acceptance 0.1 rad: fusion probability before loss  0.011 %, lost-ion energy recovery needed  99.90 %
