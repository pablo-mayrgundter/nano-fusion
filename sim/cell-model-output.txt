== nf-cell-v1: geometry ==
  site crystal: 5.00e+16 fuel pairs per cm2 per layer, 500 layers per cm, 2.50e+19 pairs per cm3 of stack
  (a fully dense crystal would hold ~3e22 per cm3; a single pair per 200 nm pixel only 1.3e+12)
  efficiencies: capture 0.2, laser 0.3, distribution 0.5  (all assumed)

== Cost of holding a site in an optical field (fuel p-11B, 2 eV light) ==
  case                                                stored [eV]   power [W]   fusions/s needed while lit
  plasmonic gap, 1 V/nm, 40 nm3, Q 10                       2.21    0.000108     2.58e+09
  plasmonic gap, 0.1 V/nm, 40 nm3, Q 10                   0.0221    1.08e-06     2.58e+07
  dielectric cavity, 1 V/nm, 125 nm3 per site, Q 1e6        6.91    3.36e-09     8.06e+04
  dielectric cavity, 0.01 V/nm, 125 nm3 per site, Q 1e6  0.000691    3.36e-13         8.06
  for comparison, the handheld tier allows 3.0e-21 W of light per fuel pair
  -> a continuously held optical field costs many orders of magnitude more than a site can repay.
