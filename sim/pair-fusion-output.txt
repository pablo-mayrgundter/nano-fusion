== (b) Nuclei on separate lattice sites: why PdD is worse than a molecule ==
  screening length that makes a caged pair match the D2 molecule: 21.6 pm
  D-D on neighbouring octahedral sites of PdD (285 pm apart, 40 meV wells), lam = 21.6 pm:  10^-88 /s
  D-D on neighbouring octahedral sites of PdD (285 pm apart, 40 meV wells), lam = 10.0 pm:  10^-68 /s
  D-D on neighbouring octahedral sites of PdD (285 pm apart, 40 meV wells), lam =  3.0 pm:  10^-48 /s
  -> distance and well stiffness cost ~10^-34 before screening enters; pairs must share a cage
